How self-scheduling works
Read this once — takes 60 seconds
1
Enter your availability
Set when you arrive and when you must leave. Only blocks that finish before your leave time will appear. Set your max games for the day.
2
Tap a block to select it
A block is 2 or 3 consecutive games on the same court. Tap any cell in a block and all games in it are selected together. Selected blocks turn dark navy.
1A  selected by you    held  another official is deciding    taken  already claimed
3
Rest slots are handled automatically
After working 3 games you must rest 2 slots. After 2 games you rest 1 slot. The grid automatically greys out those times after you select a block — you don’t need to calculate anything.
4
Confirm within 15 minutes
Once you pick a block the 15-minute hold timer starts. Select your second block if you want more games, then tap Confirm Schedule. If the timer runs out your blocks release back to the pool.
⏱ The timer is shown at the top of the page at all times.
Can I change my mind? Yes — tap a selected block or the × in the header bar to remove it. The timer resets if you remove all selections.

A block shows “held” — another official is deciding. Wait a few minutes — if they don’t confirm it releases automatically.

Need help? Contact your assigner directly.
